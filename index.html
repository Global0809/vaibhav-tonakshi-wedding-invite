Our story, across two cities.

For over a year, our story has unfolded across two cities, two time zones, and countless calls between Seattle and Delhi.

What began across the distance soon became something beautifully certain. Between late night conversations, shared laughter, visits every few months that never felt long enough, and the little ways we showed up for each other every day, we found that home could exist thousands of miles apart.

Now, we are so excited to begin the next chapter of our story together, and we would be honoured to celebrate it with the people we love most.

Until we celebrate.
